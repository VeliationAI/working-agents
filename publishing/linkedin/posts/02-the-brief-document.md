If you cannot fill this page, you are not ready to turn an agent loose. Finding that out on paper is cheaper than finding it out in a sent folder.

The page has six lines.

Job. What finished work looks like.
Output. The artifact, and where it lands. A draft is different from an email.
Tests. How you will know it is acceptable, including "unknown" when a fact is missing.
Boundaries. The tools it may use, and the ones that need your approval.
Stop. When to quit, including a step limit.
Handback. Result, checks, unknowns, sources. In that order.

I filled one in for a Thursday renewal call, in the document. Steal the blank one for a job you already do by hand.

Fluency is not one of the tests.
