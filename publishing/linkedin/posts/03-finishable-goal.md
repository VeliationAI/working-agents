"Help me prepare for the Acme meeting" is a mood. An agent will answer it with something that sounds finished. You will not be able to say whether it is.

A finishable goal has four parts.

Output. A briefing, at most two pages, as a draft. Not an email.
Tests. What they bought. Open support issues, last 90 days. Unpaid invoices. Three questions I should ask. Every fact has a source, or it says unknown.
Boundaries. Read the customer record, the inbox, and billing. Do not contact Acme.
Stop. After those three sources, or after 12 steps. Incomplete is allowed. Invented is not.

Same meeting. Second version. The loop can now be wrong in a way you can see.

A vague goal feels generous, like you are leaving room for a surprise. When you want range, ask for three angles, each with a reason, a risk, and a source. That is still a goal the loop can finish.

I would rather have an incomplete briefing on Thursday than a fluent one that invented the invoice.
